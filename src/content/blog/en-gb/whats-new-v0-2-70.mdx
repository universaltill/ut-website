Universal Till **v0.2.70** is out. Here's what changed that you'll actually
notice behind the counter — and one fix that matters if your receipts aren't in
English.

## A self-order kiosk your customers can use

Put a spare tablet or Pi on the counter and customers can order themselves. This
release adds a **dine-in / takeaway toggle** to the kiosk, so the order carries
its type through to the kitchen and the receipt — which is also what decides the
VAT rate in several of our markets.

The kiosk's basket is now fully **separate from the cashier's**. Previously a
customer tapping through a self-order could collide with a sale being rung up at
the till at the same moment. One shop, two people serving, one basket each.

## Add a second till without a network engineer

Adding another till used to mean typing addresses. Now the first-boot wizard
**discovers other tills on your own shop network** and asks to join. The existing
till shows an **approve-or-deny prompt to a manager** — pairing is never
automatic, so a device on your Wi-Fi can't quietly join itself to your sales
data.

## A manual you can actually search

`/help` is now a **two-pane searchable user manual** with screenshots that match
what you see on your own screen — they're regenerated per language, so the
Turkish manual shows the Turkish interface, not the English one.

Alongside it there's a **🐞 bug-report button** in the navigation. It captures a
screenshot of what you were looking at and sends it with the report, so
"something looked wrong on the sale screen" turns into something we can fix
without a phone call.

## Money and paperwork

- **Date-ranged Z-reports**, downloadable — pick any period rather than just
  today's close, for your accountant or your VAT return.
- **Named open tabs** (*Offene Belege*) — hold a sale under "table 4" or a
  customer's name instead of an anonymous slot.
- **Service charge is now its own field**, separate from tip, so the two are
  reported apart where the law treats them differently.
- **Pfandrückgabe** — manual bottle-deposit cash payouts, for German shops.
- **Colour-coded nested category grid** on the sale screen: fewer taps to find
  the thing you sell most.

## Fixed: receipts wrapping in the wrong place

Line wrapping on receipts and the customer display counted **bytes rather than
characters**. In English nothing looked wrong. In Turkish, Farsi, German or
Chinese, any accented or non-Latin character made the line wrap early and left
receipts looking broken. It now counts characters, as it always should have.

## Updates on your schedule

There's an **unattended auto-update toggle with a schedule** — leave it on and
tills update themselves overnight, or turn it off and stay on the version you've
tested. Your shop, your call.

## Installing

Windows has a **guided installer** and Universal Till opens in its own app
window — no terminal, no browser tab. On a Raspberry Pi, a fresh install of the
`.deb` sets up **fullscreen kiosk mode automatically on first boot**.

[Download the latest version](/download) — free, and it runs offline. If
something in this list doesn't work the way it reads, use that bug-report button
and tell us.
