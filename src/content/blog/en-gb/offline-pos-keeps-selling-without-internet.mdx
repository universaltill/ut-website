Every shop has had the moment: a queue at the counter and the router's lights
go out. With a lot of point-of-sale systems that's the end of selling until the
connection comes back. Universal Till was built the other way round. The till
keeps everything it needs **on the device itself**, so an internet outage is
something you notice on the status bar, not at the front of the queue.

Here's what that means in practice, behind the counter.

## What keeps working with no internet

Ringing up items, choosing sizes and extras, applying discounts and holding a
basket for later all run against the till's **own local data**. None of it
needs the network. A held basket that you pick up again later finishes exactly
the same way, online or not.

**Taking payment.** Cash works as normal. So does any payment method that
doesn't itself need the internet. A card reader that has to call its payment
processor is a property of that reader, not of the till: the till never blocks
a sale because the internet is down.

**Printing receipts.** The receipt printer is connected straight to the till,
by USB or over your shop's own network, so printing doesn't depend on the
internet either. If the printer itself can't be reached, the till tells you
that separately.

**Knowing what's going on.** The status bar has an **Online** light. It turns to
**Offline** the moment the device loses its network (Wi-Fi off, cable out) and
back to **Online** when it returns. When the network is fine but the internet
behind it isn't, it says **No internet**, and sales go through as usual. If the
screen itself loses touch with the till, a banner reads "Connection problem —
the till keeps working offline." It clears by itself the next time things work,
so there's nothing to dismiss.

## When the cloud side has a problem

Universal Till has optional cloud features for owners who want them, like
managing products from a browser. The till is designed so that none of them can
stop a sale:

- If the cloud refuses the till's sign-in three times in a row, a small chip
  explains why and what to do. The till **keeps selling offline**, tries again
  once an hour, and keeps everything it still has to send until it's connected
  again.
- If a paid plan can't be confirmed, or ends, the paid features pause and
  Settings explains which ones. **Selling is never affected.**
- Problem reports you capture with the bug-report button are saved on the till
  and sent once you're back online.
- The notes on what changed in each update are built into the till, so they
  open without an internet connection too.

## More than one till, no internet

If you run a [second till](/en-gb/blog/multiple-tills-one-shop/), the tills
talk to each other over your shop's own network. If an additional till can't
reach the main till, it **keeps selling anyway**. Its sales wait on that till
and sync as soon as the main till is back, and a light on the status bar says
in words what's happening: **Main till linked**, or **Main till not reachable
since … — selling offline**.

If your Wi-Fi stops the tills from seeing each other (some networks do), an
additional till can also ask your shop's cloud account where the main till last
said it was, before it falls back to selling offline. That needs both tills to
be connected to the cloud. Without it, only your shop network is searched.

## Your data stays with you

Because the till works from its own data, your sales, products and settings
live on the till in your shop. Settings → Backups makes a full snapshot any
time, and **Download** saves a copy you can keep somewhere safe, off the till.
