<h2 id="contact">Contact support</h2>

Email <a href="mailto:support@universaltill.com?subject=Support%20question">support@universaltill.com</a>. Tell us which shop and which till or app version you use, and what you expected to happen. Never send a password, a key or a card number.

We usually reply within a couple of days.

The user manual answers most day-to-day questions: see the [help pages](/en-gb/help/). For other ways to reach us, see [contact](/en-gb/contact).

<h2 id="assistant">Connect or disconnect an AI assistant</h2>

You can connect your own AI assistant account to your shop. Setup is in [Use Universal Till in Claude](/en-gb/claude/). In short:

1. In my.universaltill.com, open **Connected assistants** and choose **Connect an assistant**, then add the connector in your own assistant account and allow it on the page that opens. This connection is active once you allow it.
2. If instead you add Universal Till from the assistant's own directory, the connection stays inactive until the shop owner, or the person who allowed it, types the 6-digit activation code in my.universaltill.com. The code is valid for 24 hours.
3. The shop owner is emailed about every new connection.
4. If you do not recognise a connection, choose **Revoke** next to it. It stops working at once.

To disconnect an assistant later, open **Connected assistants**, find the connection and choose **Revoke**. How we handle the data is in the [privacy policy](/en-gb/legal/privacy/) and the [subscription terms](/en-gb/legal/subscription-terms/).

If something looks wrong with a connection and you cannot revoke it, email <a href="mailto:support@universaltill.com?subject=Support%20question">support@universaltill.com</a>.

<h2 id="security">Report a security problem</h2>

Found a vulnerability? Email <a href="mailto:security@universaltill.com?subject=Security%20report">security@universaltill.com</a>. Please do not post it publicly first. Our policy is in [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).
