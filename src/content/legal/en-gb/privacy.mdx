## What we collect, and why

**Browsing this site.** This site sets no cookies and sends nothing to
any analytics or advertising service unless you choose **Accept** on the
cookie banner. Your choice (accept or reject) is kept in your browser's
own local storage, together with your chosen display language, so the
site remembers both on your next visit; neither is sent to us or anyone
else. Our hosting provider (Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps) processes
standard web-server technical logs (for example, IP address, browser
type, and the page requested) as part of running the service, for
security and reliability, for a limited retention period it controls.

**Analytics, only if you accept.** If you accept, we use Google
Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited (with Google LLC in the
United States as its sub-processor), to understand how visitors use this
site, for example which pages are read and which countries visitors come
from, so we can improve it. Our lawful basis is your consent. Google
then sets first-party cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_<id>`, lasting up to two
years) and receives the pages you view, your device, browser and screen
details, an approximate location derived from your IP address (Google
Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses), and a random
identifier for your browser. We have turned off Google signals and ad
personalisation, and we do not use this data for advertising. Google
Analytics keeps the event-level data about your visits for two months,
after which Google deletes it; only aggregated reports (counts, not
individual visits) are kept longer. We ask again after twelve months. You can change your
mind at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page;
rejecting after you accepted stops analytics and deletes those cookies.

The [download page](/en-gb/download) makes a request directly from your
browser to GitHub's public releases API to show the current app version.
That request carries no data about you beyond what any web request
does (e.g. your IP address, visible to GitHub, not to us).

**Emailing us.** The "Join the pilot" and "Notify me" links on this site
open a pre-filled email in your own mail client, addressed to
support@universaltill.com — nothing is submitted to us through the
website itself. When you send that email (or any other email to us), we
receive and store it, including whatever you choose to tell us (for
example, your name, shop name, city, and the till system you use today),
to reply to you and, if you're joining the pilot, to set up and run the
pilot with your shop.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it with third
parties except where necessary to run our business (for example, our
email provider) or where the law requires it.

## Billing and payments

When your business buys a paid plan, Task Runner Technology Ltd (details under "Who we are") is the controller of the billing information described here. Contact: support@universaltill.com.

**What we collect and why**

- **Your business name, the account owner's email address, your billing address and, if you give it, your VAT number.** We use them to sell you the plan, send invoices and receipts, and tell you about payments and renewals. Legal basis: performing our contract with you (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We need them to sell you a plan; without them we can't.
- **Your card or bank details.** You type them only on our payment provider's page. We never see or store them. Stripe tells us only whether a payment succeeded.
- **Your acceptance of our terms.** When the account owner accepts the Subscription Terms we record the account, the version accepted, the language, the time and the IP address, as evidence of the contract. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in being able to prove what was agreed (Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep it for 6 years after the contract ends.
- **Invoices and payment records.** Kept for **6 years** after the end of the financial year they belong to, because UK tax law requires it. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
- **Billing events.** A short technical log of payment events, with no card details, for up to 35 days, so each payment is applied to your account exactly once. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in billing you correctly (Art. 6(1)(f)).

**Who receives it**

- **Stripe** takes your payments, holds your card or bank details, creates your invoices and runs the "Manage billing" page. Stripe acts for us for billing and invoicing, and on its own account for fraud prevention and its own legal duties: see [stripe.com/gb/privacy](https://stripe.com/gb/privacy).
- **Microsoft Azure** hosts our systems in France.
- **Brevo** delivers our emails.

**Where it goes**

Our own copy is stored in the European Union, which UK law treats as giving adequate protection. Stripe may process your data in the United States and other countries, under the transfer safeguards set out in its data processing agreement with us.

**Emails about billing**

Receipts, invoices, failed-payment notices and renewal reminders are part of the service. You get them even if you have said no to marketing.

We make no automated decisions about you with this data. Your rights, and how to complain to the ICO, are under "Your rights" below.

certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the UK
Extension to it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you're located, data protection law (including UK
GDPR and, for EU visitors, the EU GDPR) may give you the right to access,
correct, delete, or export the personal data we hold about you, and to
object to or restrict how we use it. To exercise any of these, email
[support@universaltill.com](mailto:support@universaltill.com). If you're
not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your local data
protection authority — in the UK, the
[Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).
