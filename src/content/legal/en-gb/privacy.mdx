## What we collect, and why

**Browsing this site.** This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics
or advertising trackers of any kind — there is nothing here to opt out
of. It does store one thing in your browser's own local storage, never
sent to us or anyone else: your chosen display language, so the site
remembers it on your next visit. Our hosting provider (Microsoft Azure
Static Web Apps) processes standard web-server technical logs (for
example, IP address, browser type, and the page requested) as part of
running the service, for security and reliability, for a limited
retention period it controls.

The [download page](/en-gb/download) makes a request directly from your
browser to GitHub's public releases API to show the current app version.
That request carries no data about you beyond what any web request
does (e.g. your IP address, visible to GitHub, not to us).

**Emailing us.** The "Join the pilot" and "Notify me" links on this site
open a pre-filled email in your own mail client, addressed to
support@universaltill.com — nothing is submitted to us through the
website itself. When you send that email (or any other email to us), we
receive and store it, including whatever you choose to tell us (for
example, your name, shop name, city, and the till system you use today),
to reply to you and, if you're joining the pilot, to set up and run the
pilot with your shop.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it with third
parties except where necessary to run our business (for example, our
email provider) or where the law requires it.

## Retention

We keep pilot and enquiry emails for as long as reasonably needed to
manage our relationship with you, then delete or anonymise them.

## International transfers

Where a service we use to receive or store your email operates outside
the UK or European Economic Area, we rely on that provider's own
safeguards (such as the standard contractual clauses) for the transfer.

## Your rights

Depending on where you're located, data protection law (including UK
GDPR and, for EU visitors, the EU GDPR) may give you the right to access,
correct, delete, or export the personal data we hold about you, and to
object to or restrict how we use it. To exercise any of these, email
[support@universaltill.com](mailto:support@universaltill.com). If you're
not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your local data
protection authority — in the UK, the
[Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).
