## What we collect, and why

**Browsing this site.** This site sets no cookies and sends nothing to
any analytics or advertising service unless you choose **Accept** on the
cookie banner. Your choice (accept or reject) is kept in your browser's
own local storage, together with your chosen display language, so the
site remembers both on your next visit; neither is sent to us or anyone
else. Our hosting provider (Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps) processes
standard web-server technical logs (for example, IP address, browser
type, and the page requested) as part of running the service, for
security and reliability, for a limited retention period it controls.

**Analytics, only if you accept.** If you accept, we use Google
Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited (with Google LLC in the
United States as its sub-processor), to understand how visitors use this
site, for example which pages are read and which countries visitors come
from, so we can improve it. Our lawful basis is your consent. Google
then sets first-party cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_<id>`, lasting up to two
years) and receives the pages you view, your device, browser and screen
details, an approximate location derived from your IP address (Google
Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses), and a random
identifier for your browser. We have turned off Google signals and ad
personalisation, and we do not use this data for advertising. Google
Analytics keeps the event-level data about your visits for two months,
after which Google deletes it; only aggregated reports (counts, not
individual visits) are kept longer. We ask again after twelve months. You can change your
mind at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page;
rejecting after you accepted stops analytics and deletes those cookies.

The [download page](/en-gb/download) makes a request directly from your
browser to GitHub's public releases API to show the current app version.
That request carries no data about you beyond what any web request
does (e.g. your IP address, visible to GitHub, not to us).

**Emailing us.** The "Join the pilot" and "Notify me" links on this site
open a pre-filled email in your own mail client, addressed to
support@universaltill.com — nothing is submitted to us through the
website itself. When you send that email (or any other email to us), we
receive and store it, including whatever you choose to tell us (for
example, your name, shop name, city, and the till system you use today),
to reply to you and, if you're joining the pilot, to set up and run the
pilot with your shop.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it with third
parties except where necessary to run our business (for example, our
email provider) or where the law requires it.

## Billing and payments

When your business buys a paid plan, Task Runner Technology Ltd (details under "Who we are") is the controller of the billing information described here. Contact: support@universaltill.com.

**What we collect and why**

- **Your business name, the account owner's email address, your billing address and, if you give it, your VAT number.** We use them to sell you the plan, send invoices and receipts, and tell you about payments and renewals. Legal basis: performing our contract with you (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We need them to sell you a plan; without them we can't.
- **Your card or bank details.** You type them only on our payment provider's page. We never see or store them. Stripe tells us only whether a payment succeeded.
- **Your acceptance of our terms.** When the account owner accepts the Subscription Terms we record the account, the version accepted, the language, the time and the IP address, as evidence of the contract. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in being able to prove what was agreed (Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep it for at least 6 years after the contract ends, and delete it when it is no longer needed to prove what was agreed.
- **Invoices and payment records.** Kept for **6 years** after the end of the financial year they belong to, because UK tax law requires it. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
- **Billing events.** A short technical log of payment events, with no card details, for up to 35 days, so each payment is applied to your account exactly once. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in billing you correctly (Art. 6(1)(f)).

**Who receives it**

- **Stripe** takes your payments, holds your card or bank details, creates your invoices and runs the "Manage billing" page. Stripe acts for us for billing and invoicing, and on its own account for fraud prevention and its own legal duties: see [stripe.com/gb/privacy](https://stripe.com/gb/privacy).
- **Microsoft Azure** hosts our systems in France.
- **Brevo** delivers our emails.

**Where it goes**

Our own copy is stored in the European Union, which UK law treats as giving adequate protection. Stripe may process your data in the United States and other countries, under the transfer safeguards set out in its data processing agreement with us.

**Emails about billing**

Receipts, invoices, failed-payment notices and renewal reminders are part of the service. You get them even if you have said no to marketing.

We make no automated decisions about you with this data. Your rights, and how to complain to us or to the regulator, are under "Your rights" below.

## The Universal Till cloud service

This section is for shop owners, their staff and the people they invite, when a till connects to our cloud or someone uses my.universaltill.com. Task Runner Technology Ltd (details under "Who we are") is the controller of the data described here. Contact: support@universaltill.com.

**Your shop's own business data is different.** The products, sales, staff accounts and customer details your tills put into our cloud belong to the shop. For the personal data in them the shop is the controller and we only process it for the shop, under our [Data Processing Agreement](/en-gb/legal/dpa/). If you are a shop's staff member or customer, ask the shop about that data first.

**What we collect and why**

- **Your Universal Till ID and account.** Your name and email address, and the shops you own or belong to. We use them to sign you in, run your account and send you service messages. Legal basis: performing our contract with your business (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); for staff and invited people, our legitimate interest in letting the shop give them access (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Sign-in.** Our own sign-in service (Universal Till ID) records your sign-ins and sessions: the time, IP address and browser. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure (Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep sign-in records while you have an account.
- **Invitations.** We receive an invited person's email address from the shop owner who invites them. We keep the invited email address, sealed with the shop's key, to send the invitation and check the sign-in. An invitation that was revoked, expired or used is deleted after **30 days**; the accepted one of a current member stays while that person is in the shop. Legal basis: legitimate interest in letting the shop give people access (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **What a till sends when it connects.** When a till registers with our cloud it sends the shop's name, the till's name, a random identifier for the till, the software version, the device system, and the region and country set on it. When it syncs it also sends the language it is set to, and we note when it last connected. We use this to run the service, to support you and to protect the service from abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). A shop's name can be a person's name; that is why we treat this as personal data.
- **Connection addresses.** When a till registers, pairs, redeems a set-up code or checks in, we record the IP address it connects from, a short history of the addresses each till has used, and the country and city each address belongs to. We work out that location ourselves from a local copy of a public IP location database, so the address is never sent to anyone else for it. IPv6 addresses are shortened to their network part. We use this only to prevent fraud, to meet our sanctions obligations and to support you. History entries are deleted after **90 days**; the most recent address stays while the till is registered. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for sanctions checks; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in preventing fraud and supporting our customers. IP location data by [DB-IP.com](https://db-ip.com) (CC BY 4.0).
- **Your acceptance of our terms.** When you accept the Merchant Terms of Service, on a till or on my.universaltill.com, we record the version, the language, the time and where it was accepted, as evidence of the contract. We keep it for at least 6 years after the contract ends, and delete it when it is no longer needed to prove what was agreed. Legal basis: legitimate interest in being able to prove what was agreed (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Address search and map.** When you search for your shop's address or view the map on my.universaltill.com, the text you type and the map area you view are sent through our server to Photon (run by Komoot) and OpenStreetMap. Please don't type a private person's address there. Legal basis: legitimate interest in letting you place your shop on the map (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Security records.** Our service logs, kept for **14 days**. The record of important actions in your shop (who did what, when, and from which IP address) is kept for the shop for **365 days** under the Data Processing Agreement; we also read it to keep the service secure and investigate misuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and fixing faults (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Support.** What you tell us when you email support. We use it to help you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)). Support emails are kept as set out under Retention below. A problem report sent from the till, with its attachments, is held for the shop under the Data Processing Agreement.

We need the account details and what a till sends to provide the service; without them we can't connect a till or run an account. We make no automated decisions about you with this data.

**How long we keep it, overall.** Account details are kept while your business has an account with us. When a shop is deleted, we erase its data 30 days later, except records the law requires us to keep (such as archived fiscal and tax reports, which we keep for their legal retention period) and the acceptance and billing records above. Backup copies expire on their own within 90 days.

**Who receives it**

- **Microsoft Azure** hosts the service in France, with a copy of database backups in Azure's paired region; the keys that protect sealed data are kept with Microsoft Azure in the United Kingdom.
- **Photon (Komoot) and OpenStreetMap** receive address searches and map requests from my.universaltill.com.
- **Brevo** delivers our emails.
- **GitHub** holds our team's private tracker. A problem report reaches it only when our support team files it there to fix a fault.
- We keep a backup copy of the database on our own equipment in the United Kingdom.

**Where it goes**

Our cloud copy is stored in the European Union, which UK law treats as giving adequate protection. GitHub is in the United States. We rely on the transfer safeguards GitHub offers: the standard contractual clauses or, where GitHub is certified, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

**Objecting.** Where we rely on our legitimate interest, you can object at any time by emailing support@universaltill.com, and we stop unless we have compelling grounds or need the data for a legal claim.

**Usage statistics and feedback.** By default the till sends us no usage statistics or feedback. If a shop turns on the plugin status report, the till sends our marketplace the names, versions and on/off state of its plugins, with the till's and the shop's identifiers. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping plugins working, at the shop's choice (Art. 6(1)(f)). If we offer wider usage statistics, they will be off unless the shop says yes, nothing will name your shop, staff or customers, and we will update this policy first. We have set these limits for them: a till's random counting code is kept for no more than 14 days, combined totals for 13 months, feedback comments for 12 months, and the record of a shop's yes or no for 12 months after it says no.

## Your rights

Depending on where you're located, data protection law (including UK
GDPR and, for EU visitors, the EU GDPR) may give you the right to access,
correct, delete, or export the personal data we hold about you, and to
object to or restrict how we use it. To exercise any of these, email
[support@universaltill.com](mailto:support@universaltill.com).

**Complaints.** You have the right to complain to us about how we use
your personal data. Email
[support@universaltill.com](mailto:support@universaltill.com). If your
message is about how we use your personal data, we treat it as a data
protection complaint. We acknowledge it within 30 days and tell
you the outcome without undue delay. You can also complain to your local
data protection authority at any time. In the UK that is the
[Information Commission](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) (formerly
the Information Commissioner's Office).
