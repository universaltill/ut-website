## 1. Who this agreement is between

1.1 This agreement is between your business ("you") and TASK RUNNER TECHNOLOGY LTD, trading as Universal Till ("we", "us"), company number 11442274, registered office 82 A James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Suffolk IP28 7DE, United Kingdom. Contact: support@universaltill.com.

1.2 It forms part of our [Merchant Terms of Service](/en-gb/legal/merchant-terms/). You accept it when you accept those terms, and it lasts as long as we hold personal data for you under them.

1.3 It applies to personal data that we process **for you** when you use the Universal Till cloud service. For that data you are the **controller** and we are your **processor**. It is the contract that Article 28 of the UK GDPR and, where it applies to you, of the EU GDPR requires. Where a word in this agreement has a meaning in that law (such as "personal data", "processing" or "personal data breach"), it has that meaning here.

## 2. Where we are a controller, not your processor

2.1 For some data we decide ourselves why and how it is used. For that data we are a **controller** in our own right, this agreement does not apply, and our [privacy policy](/en-gb/legal/privacy/) explains what we do. It covers:

- your account, sign-in and billing details, and your acceptance of our terms;
- what a till sends us when it registers or checks in with our cloud (such as its name, version, system, country and language, and when it last connected), and the connection addresses we record, which we use to run and secure the service, to support you and to meet our sanctions duties;
- security, fraud-prevention and service logs;
- your messages to our support team;
- if we offer them and you choose to share them: usage statistics from your tills that don't name your shop, staff or customers, and feedback you send us from the till.

2.2 We use that data only for the purposes set out in our privacy policy. It is not your business data, and you do not instruct us about it.

## 3. What we process for you

3.1 **Subject matter and purpose.** We store, sync, back up, display and send the data your tills and your users put into our cloud, only to provide the cloud service you use under the Merchant Terms: cloud sync, browser management on my.universaltill.com, the cloud archive, reports, integrations you switch on, and support.

3.2 **Duration.** For as long as the Merchant Terms apply, and afterwards until we delete the data under clause 9.

3.3 **People the data is about.**

- your staff who use the till or my.universaltill.com (owners, managers, cashiers) and people you invite to your shop;
- your customers, where your tills send their details to our cloud (for example an order-tracking reference, or a customer who appears in a problem report you send us).

3.4 **Kinds of personal data.**

- staff accounts: user name, display name, role and whether a PIN is set; a PIN set from my.universaltill.com is sealed to the till's own key and removed once the till has applied it;
- a pseudonymous staff identifier in sales totals and archived reports;
- invitees' email addresses, sealed with your shop's key;
- till and device identifiers linked to your shop, the till's address on your local network and the devices connected to it;
- customer order-tracking references and receipt numbers;
- problem reports you choose to send us, with any screenshots, recordings or logs you attach, which can show people in your shop;
- for a sales channel you connect, the channel's own reference for the customer and the customer's country on each order;
- device and application log lines;
- the identity of the person who made a change, in your shop's audit log.

3.5 We don't intend to process special categories of personal data (such as health data). A problem report you send can contain them by accident; please don't record people in it if you can avoid it. We never process payment card data: card payments are handled by your payment provider.

## 4. Our duties as your processor

4.1 **Your instructions.** We process the personal data only on your documented instructions. Your instructions are these terms and the way you set up and use the service. That includes transfers outside the UK and the European Economic Area (clause 7). If the law requires us to process it in another way, we tell you first, unless the law forbids that. If we think an instruction breaks data protection law, we tell you straight away.

4.2 **Confidentiality.** Only people who need access to provide the service get it, and they are bound to keep the data confidential.

4.3 **Security.** We take the measures that Article 32 requires, appropriate to the risk. The measures we use today are in Annex 2.

4.4 **Help with people's requests.** If someone asks to see, correct, delete or move their data, or objects to its use, we help you answer, as far as we reasonably can. If they ask us directly, we pass the request to you and don't answer it ourselves unless you ask us to.

4.5 **Help with your other duties.** We help you, as far as we reasonably can and given the information we have, with security, personal data breach notifications, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with a regulator (Articles 32 to 36).

4.6 **Breaches.** If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting the data we process for you, we tell you without undue delay, by email to the address on your account, or in the till if we have no address for you. We tell you what we know (what happened, the data and people likely affected, the likely consequences and what we are doing about it) and add more as we learn it.

5.3 We use only sub-processors that are bound by a data processing agreement giving your data at least the protection of this agreement. We remain fully responsible to you for what our sub-processors do.

5.4 Services that **you** choose to connect (for example your payment provider, a delivery, booking or accounting platform, or an AI assistant you connect to your shop) are not our sub-processors. You choose them and your contract with them is your own.

## 6. Where the data is

6.1 We store your cloud data with Microsoft Azure in France (European Union). Azure also keeps a copy of the database backups in its paired region. The keys that protect sealed personal fields are held with Microsoft Azure in the United Kingdom. We keep a backup copy of the database on our own equipment in the United Kingdom.

6.2 We tell you in advance, as in clause 5.2, before we move where your data is stored.

## 7. Transfers

7.1 Transfers between the UK and the European Economic Area rely on the adequacy decisions each side has made for the other.

7.2 Where a sub-processor in Annex 3 processes data in another country, we make the transfer only with the safeguards the law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU standard contractual clauses, or an adequacy regulation or decision.

## 8. Information and audits

8.1 We give you the information you reasonably need to show that we meet this agreement. Ask at support@universaltill.com.

8.2 If that information isn't enough, or a regulator asks, you or an independent auditor bound to confidentiality may audit us. Give us at least 30 days' notice. Audits take place no more than once in 12 months (unless after a breach or at a regulator's request), during working hours, and in a way that doesn't expose other customers' data. Each side pays its own costs.

## 9. When the service ends

9.1 You can ask us at any time for an export of the personal data we hold for you, and we provide it within 30 days of your request.

9.2 You can ask us to delete it. We delete it from our live systems within 30 days of your request, unless the law requires us to keep it. That includes any problem report of yours that our support team filed in our tracker.

9.3 Until you choose, we keep it as the Merchant Terms describe (clauses 7 and 20 there, and clause 9 of the Subscription Terms), and you can still ask for an export or deletion.

9.4 **What we keep.** Records the law requires us to keep, such as archived fiscal and tax reports, stay for their legal retention period and are then deleted. Backup copies are not deleted one by one: they expire on their own within 90 days.

## Annex 2. Security measures

- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between tills, browsers and our cloud.
- Every request for your shop's data is checked against your shop, centrally, so one shop can't read another's.
- Personal fields that we never need to search (such as invitees' email addresses) are sealed with a key that belongs to your shop. When we erase your shop's data we destroy that key, which makes the sealed copies, including those in backups, unreadable.
- We keep no readable staff PIN: a PIN set from my.universaltill.com is sealed to the till's own key.
- Nightly database copies in Azure are written by a process that can create them but not change or delete them. Backups expire automatically (clause 9.4).
- Access to production systems is limited to the people who run the service.
- Service logs are kept for 14 days.
- Your tills keep working offline, so an outage of our cloud doesn't stop you trading or lose the data on your till.
