## 1. Who may publish

1.1 A publisher is a person or organisation with a verified publisher identity in the marketplace. Before a listing is shown, we check who the publisher is and record an accountable legal entity (a company, or an individual who answers for the listing in their own name).

1.2 You must give true details, keep them up to date, and tell us if the person or company behind a listing changes. A listing may not be published under a false or borrowed identity.

## 2. What you must do

2.1 Every listing and every release goes through our review before shoppers or shops can install it. We may refuse or remove a release that fails review.

2.2 You are responsible for your listing, its code and its text. Your listing must follow the [content guidelines](/en-gb/legal/marketplace-content-guidelines/), the law that applies to it, and the terms of any service it connects to.

2.3 You must describe truthfully what your listing does, which data it reads or sends, and which permissions it needs. A change that adds data collection or a new permission needs a new release and a fresh review.

2.4 You must answer a notice from us within a reasonable time, and keep a working contact address in your publisher profile.

## 4. Reports and what we do with them

4.1 Anyone can report a listing with the **Report this listing** link on its page. A report names the listing, a reason and what is wrong; a contact address is optional.

4.2 We read each report. We may ask the reporter or the publisher for more information, and we may act before we have finished looking where there is a risk of harm.

4.3 Where a report is upheld we may: ask the publisher to fix the listing, hide it, remove a release, withdraw the listing, revoke its signing so installed copies stop, or close the publisher's account. Where we can, we tell the publisher what we did and why.
