This policy is part of our [Subscription Terms](/en-gb/legal/subscription-terms/). We sell paid plans to businesses. If the law treats you as a consumer, nothing here removes the rights it gives you.

## Cancel any time

- Cancel under **Manage billing** on my.universaltill.com, or email support@universaltill.com from the account owner's address.
- Your plan stays active to the end of the period you have paid for, then moves to the free Local plan. Your tills keep selling.
- No minimum term, no cancellation fee.

## 14-day money-back on your first payment

- If you are not happy, email us within **14 days of your first paid invoice** and we refund it in full. We then cancel your plan straight away and your account moves to the Local plan.
- This applies once per business, to the first paid invoice of its first paid plan. If you bought during a free trial, the 14 days start on the day we took the first payment.

## When we always refund

We refund the amount concerned if:

- we charged you twice or charged the wrong amount;
- we end your subscription without your fault (Subscription Terms 12.1): the unused part of the period;
- a paid cloud feature is unavailable for more than 7 days in a row because of a fault on our side and you cancel: the unused part of the period;
- an integration named in your plan's description stops working for good because the other company withdrew it, and you cancel within 30 days of our telling you: the unused part of the period.

## Otherwise

We don't refund part of a period, and we don't refund renewals.

## How refunds are paid

- To the payment method you paid with. We aim to issue it within 10 working days of agreeing it. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
- We send you a VAT credit note for the refunded amount.
- We don't deduct processing fees from a refund.

**Please talk to us before disputing a charge with your bank.** We will usually sort it out faster, and a bank dispute can end your plan (Subscription Terms 12.3).
