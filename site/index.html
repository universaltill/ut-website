The point of sale that's actually yours.

Free, open source, and offline-first — a full sale completes with no internet at all. Runs on a Raspberry Pi, an old laptop, or a proper touch terminal. No monthly fee for the core. Ever.

No lock-in. Of any kind.

No contracts or proprietary hardware

Big-name POS systems tie you into multi-year contracts and terminals that only work with them. Universal Till runs on generic hardware you already own — a complete DIY till costs from about £70.

No payment processor lock-in

We never sit in your money flow. Use any card machine and any processor you like — so rising per-transaction fees and frozen payouts simply can't happen to you here.

Your data, exportable, always

Sales, catalog, everything lives in a database you own and can export any time. Switching away is easy — which is exactly why you won't need to.

Everything a real shop needs, free

Offline-first checkout

Internet down? Keep selling. The till never blocks a sale on the network and syncs when the connection returns.

Fast, touch-first selling

Barcode scanning, product tiles, hold & resume, split payments, returns, shifts and cash drawer counts.

Inventory & catalog

Stock levels, receipts and adjustments with a manager-PIN audit trail, product images, price history.

Reports

Sales by day, top items, payment breakdown — on the till, no cloud required.

Any language, including RTL

Fully translated interface with right-to-left support (Farsi, Arabic…), language packs as plugins, and an editor to reword any text on the till.

PIN login & roles

Cashier, manager, and admin roles with a device-wide lockout and a full audit log.

Plugin marketplace

Themes, payment methods, languages, pages and more — every plugin is signature-verified before it runs.

AI where it helps, on your terms

Camera product identification runs against a model you host — open models on your own machine. No mandatory AI, no per-call fees.

Receipts & printing

Clean printable receipts in every language, thermal printer support on the roadmap's front burner.

Run it on almost anything

Raspberry Pi

A Pi 4 makes an excellent dedicated till — kiosk mode included, 3D-printable enclosure plans in the docs.

Used thin client or old PC

A £15–45 second-hand thin client runs it comfortably. The whole POS is one small program.

Windows, macOS, Linux

Download and run. A guided setup wizard and one-click installers are in active development.

Growing plugin ecosystem

Payment methods, themes, language packs, info pages and buttons ship today through a signed marketplace. Delivery-platform integration, loyalty, gift cards, kitchen displays and a customer app are on the public roadmap — the core stays free while optional cloud services keep the lights on.
