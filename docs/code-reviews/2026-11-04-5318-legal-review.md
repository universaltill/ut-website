## Findings, ranked
1. **Medium / likely - Page links to privacy policy and terms that do not cover the connector yet.** ADR 12.7 gate 3 requires a service privacy notice covering the connector (#5325, unmerged). The EN privacy page mentions no Anthropic/connector flow. The page was made self-sufficient on the one point that matters (what is sent, to whom, merchant responsibility) and does not claim the linked pages cover it. DE/TR links fall back to the English text. **Flag: do not publish before #5325 or owner accepts the gap.**
2. **Medium / likely - Missing controller and transfer duties (UK GDPR Art. 13/14, 28; KVKK 6698 art. 9, unverified section).** Fixed with one bullet in each locale: Anthropic may process outside UK/EU; if customer/staff data is switched on, the shop is responsible for a lawful basis and informing people. Processor/DPA wording for our own role belongs in #5325.
3. **Low / possible - Translations did not name English as governing** (legal skill rule). Added a closing line in all three locales.
4. **Low - Trademark:** nominative use only, non-affiliation and trademark-owner disclaimer present in all locales; no logo use. OK. DE/TR wording checked; "Inhaber", "Kassenkraft", "kasiyer", "iptal" read correctly (not a native-lawyer check).
5. **Low - "Nothing from a shop on another server region is sent"** is true today (single listed host, ADR 12.1) but a second plane is undecided; revisit then.
6. **security.txt (RFC 9116):** Contact (mailto) and Expires (RFC 3339, under 1 year) present; Canonical, Preferred-Languages fine; validator passes. Gaps, low: no `Policy`/`Acknowledgments` (optional); expiry 2027-05-01 needs a renewal reminder; Canonical host www must be the host that serves it.
