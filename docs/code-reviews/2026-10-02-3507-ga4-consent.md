# Review — GA4 behind cookie consent (ut-docs#3507)

**Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Built by:** Claude Opus 5.5 · **Reviewed by:** Claude Fable (independent subagent, read-only)

## What shipped
- `site/consent.js`: cookie banner + footer "Cookie settings". Google Analytics 4 (`G-6WZY1CZ94Q`) is injected only after Accept (basic consent mode: nothing reaches Google before a choice). Consent Mode v2 has every `ad_*` denied and `analytics_storage` granted, with Google signals and ad personalisation off. The choice is kept in `localStorage.ut_consent` with a timestamp and expires after 12 months. Reject sets `ga-disable-<id>` and deletes `_ga*` cookies on the host and every parent domain.
- Loaded `defer` after `i18n.js` on all `site/*.html` and in `BaseLayout.astro`. Strings are in all 5 locales.
- CSP (`scripts/generate-csp.js`): `script-src` gains the single exact host `https://www.googletagmanager.com`; `img-src`/`connect-src` gain Google's documented GA4 list (`*.google-analytics.com`, `*.googletagmanager.com`, plus `*.analytics.google.com` for connect).
- The privacy policy now describes GA4: consent basis, cookies, data sent, retention, transfers (DPF + UK Extension) and withdrawal.
- `tests/consent.spec.js` (20 tests), `tests/csp.spec.js` regex, README.

## Findings
| # | Sev | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | Reject after Accept only pushed a consent update, so the loaded gtag kept sending cookieless pings (enhanced measurement). | Fixed: `ga-disable-<id>` set on withdraw; test added. |
| 2 | Low | Accept → Reject → Accept on one page stayed denied. | Fixed: re-accept pushes the update and clears the flag; test added. |
| 3 | Low | Consent never expired. | Fixed: 12-month expiry; test added; policy says so. |
| 4 | Low | The policy named no retention period. | Fixed: states two months (the GA4 default) with aggregated reports kept longer. **The owner must not raise retention in the GA console without updating the policy.** |
| 5 | Low | The withdrawal test used host-only localhost cookies, so the parent-domain loop was never exercised. | Fixed: the test serves the site as `www.universaltill.com` with `.universaltill.com` cookies. |
| 6 | Low | Focus dropped to `<body>` after closing a reopened banner. | Fixed: focus returns to the footer button; asserted. |
